Recipe

Smoked chuck roast

The poor man’s brisket

MMarcus put this up Sunday

Cut
Chuck, 3–4 lb
Heat
250°F
Time
6–7 hr
Wood
Post oak

“Half the price of brisket and done by dinner. Don’t trim much. The fat is the point.”Marcus

I made this Copy as text

Ingredients Serves 6

  • Chuck roast, 3 to 4 lb1
  • Kosher salt2 tbsp
  • Coarse black pepper2 tbsp
  • Granulated garlic1 tsp
  • Yellow mustard, to bind2 tbsp
  • Beef stock, for the wrap½ cup

Method

  1. Salt the roast the night before and leave it uncovered in the fridge.
  2. Bring the smoker to 250°F with post oak. Slather the roast with mustard, then coat it with the pepper and garlic.
  3. Smoke fat side up until the bark sets and the middle reads 165°F, about 4 hours.
  4. Wrap it tight in butcher paper with the stock. Put it back on until a probe slides in like soft butter, around 203°F.
  5. Rest it in a dry cooler for an hour. Slice against the grain, or pull it.
